userFLOW v0.2.17 updater behavior:
- Download and SHA-256 verification stay inside userFLOW.
- When the update reaches ready-install, userFLOW automatically opens the verified Windows installer.
- The legacy Install update button stays hidden during the normal path.
- If Windows rejects the installer launch, the legacy button is revealed as a manual fallback.
- The old userFLOW process quits only after the installer process has actually spawned.
